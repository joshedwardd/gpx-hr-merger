HR Merger

phone GPS × watch heart rate → Strava GPX

Load the accurate GPS track from your phone and the heart-rate recording from your watch. The tool lines them up by timestamp, writes the watch's HR (and cadence, if present) onto every phone trackpoint, and exports one clean GPX for Strava. Accepts .gpx and .tcx for either side. Everything runs in your browser — no file ever leaves your device.

+0 s Nudge if the two clocks drift. The coverage bar below tells you when it fits.
Load both files to see the merge.
Getting the two files & uploading:
1. Record the run with GPS on your phone app and the same run on your watch — start them close together so the clocks line up.
2. Phone: export the activity as GPX (it must include <time> stamps).
3. Watch: in the Zepp app open the workout, then share/export as GPX or TCX. If it already auto-synced to Strava, open that Strava activity and use Export Original instead.
4. Merge here, download, upload the result to Strava, and delete the duplicate watch-synced activity if there is one.
