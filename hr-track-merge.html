Getting the two files & uploading:
1. Record your run with GPS on your phone app and the same run on your watch — start them close together so the clocks line up.
2. Phone: export the activity as GPX (it must include <time> stamps).
3. Watch: in the Zepp app open the workout → share/export as GPX or TCX. If Zepp already auto-synced it to Strava, you can instead open that Strava activity → Export Original.
4. Merge here, download, and upload the result to Strava. If the watch version already synced, delete that Strava activity so you don't get a duplicate (or turn off Zepp→Strava auto-sync and only upload merged files).
